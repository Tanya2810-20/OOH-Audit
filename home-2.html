Our Value Proposition

Objective. Transparent. Accountable.

Gain full visibility into your out-of-home campaigns. From physical site checks to tamper-proof reports, every part of the process is built to confirm that your ads are exactly where they should be—live, visible, and documented.

  • Proof of Performance

    Get on-ground report from field executives and confirm your ads are live and displayed as planned. No guesswork, just field-verified proof.

  • Real-time Visual Reports

    View campaign data as it comes in. Access dashboards with images, field activity, and campaign status from each site.

  • Geo-tagged Evidence

    Every image is embedded with GPS coordinates, time & date stamp, and includes full address details, so you know exactly where and when it was captured.

  • Tamper-proof Technology

    Our applications built with tamper-proof mechanism, ensure data cannot be edited once it’s uploaded, so what you see is the original, unedited proof straight from the ground.

  • Custom Reports

    Generate reports by location, date range, or campaign. Export in PDF or Excel format for easy sharing with teams, vendors, or stakeholders.

  • Works Everywhere

    Whether it’s a metro city, tier-2 town, or a remote village, our system is designed to work in all environments, even offline in low-connectivity areas.

    How it works

    Define the Mandate

    Your team will share the campaign mandate, list of locations, coordinates, and guidelines. This serves as the operational blueprint for our field execution.

    Deployment of Ground Team

    We dispatch our field agents to the assigned locations. Each team member uses the Moshpit App for on-ground audits, equipped with campaign details and site-specific instructions. Agents capture proof of performance and upload geo-tagged photos directly from the field.

    Real-Time Monitoring

    Field teams upload geo-tagged photos and site updates using the Moshpit App. These updates are accessible instantly on the Brand Manager app and dashboard, giving your team real-time access to campaign images, location data, and audit activity.

    Review Consolidated Reports

    At the end of the audit period, you receive well-structured reports in PDF or Excel formats. These reports include agent logs, flagged locations, compliance status, and other key insights.

    Tailored to Your Needs

    We can configure the process to match how your team works—report formats, frequency, dashboards, or approval flows.
