Care Insurance

Nationwide Retail Branding Campaign Success

We supported a leading health insurance company in auditing 1,600+ sites for a pan-India campaign. Using our geospatial tools and tamper-proof technology, the client flagged issues like uninstalled screens and poor ad visibility. Through comprehensive audits, the client was able to identify key areas of non-performance and take immediate corrective actions.

M3M Builders

Campaign Audit & Compliance Story

M3M Builders, one of India’s leading real estate developers, needed better control over their OOH branding campaigns across Delhi-NCR. Using our platform, they conducted regular audits and identified issues like unauthorized media and poor installs. Results: 100% visibility, better brand consistency, and higher ROI.

Read the Full Case Study

Siggnature

Nationwide Ooh Advertising Campaign

Siggnature launched a large-scale OOH advertising campaign across 700+ sites. To ensure consistency and performance, the client used our platform to conduct audits capturing geo-tagged images and generating real-time visual reports. These audits uncovered major operational gaps. This led to a shift to weekly reviews, reducing non-compliance, resulting in improved execution and better visibility across the campaign.

Bubna Advertising

OOH Media Monitoring & Compliance

We worked with Bubna Advertising to build an audit & monitoring system for their OOH media campaigns. The client, managing over 150,000 outdoor media sites across India, needed a reliable way to verify campaign execution and field activity. We developed a solution that combined a custom-built mobile app for audit & verification, and a centralized dashboard to track compliance, reduce pilferage, and ensure accountability.
