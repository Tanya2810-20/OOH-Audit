Independent Audit and Media Monitoring Services

Drive campaign performance with trusted, tamper-proof, field-verified location data. Achieve your objectives, ensure compliance, prevent pilferage, and execute your Out-of-Home campaigns with full transparency and efficiency.

  1. Home
  2. Services

We’ve engineered proprietary software and applications purpose-built to monitor, manage, and audit Out-of-Home campaigns at scale. Each solution is designed for precision, operational consistency, and complete transparency—ensuring your campaigns are executed efficiently, verified thoroughly, and protected against loss or error.

Ooh Campaign Monitoring

Track & monitor your OOH assets across locations with geo-tagged, time-stamped images and real-time updates from the ground. Know what’s live, what’s not, and when it went up.

Learn More

Media Asset Tracker

Track all static and digital media assets deployed across locations. Get real-time updates on their status, visibility, and condition—ensuring nothing goes unnoticed or unreported.

Learn More

Transit Media Tracker

Audit media placed on buses, metros, trains, and other moving vehicles. Use geo-tagged photos to confirm placements, monitor routes, and maintain consistency across all campaigns.

Learn More

Geotagging Services

Capture geographical metadata, accurate coordinates, full address info, embed them directly into each asset image. Ideal for maintaining accurate location records, audit trails, and improving campaign planning.

Learn More

Branding Recce and Data Collection

Plan smarter campaigns with location-specific insights, consumer behaviour & intent, capture on-ground data, and analyse traffic across multiple campaign locations.

Learn More

Retail Branding Activity Tracking

Verify in-store branding, POP displays, promotional material deployment, and retail activations across chains and standalone outlets

Learn More

BTL Campaign Tracking

Track Below-the-Line activations across cities, event branding, kiosks, mall branding, vehicle wraps, and more with verified on-ground visual reports.

Learn More

Proof of Performance for Retail Branding Campaigns

Track campaign execution across multiple locations—storefronts, shelves, counters, and more. Get visual proof, monitor activity, and ensure brand consistency at every touchpoint.

Learn More

Physical Asset Audit & Monitoring

Get complete visibility of your deployed media—hoardings, billboards, kiosks, and more with accurate proof of performance and location-based proof of deployment.

Learn More

Wall Painting Monitoring

Track and manage wall painting campaigns across urban and remote locations. Ensure quality, consistency, and compliance while minimizing wear, damage, and execution gaps.

Learn More

Warehouse Audit

Audit storage units, verify site conditions, and inspect inventory with geo-tagged photo evidence. Ensure accurate location tracking and maintain compliance with operational standards.

Learn More

Pre-Acquisition Audit

We conduct a physical verification of media assets to validate their existence, condition, and visibility. This audit ensures that every site listed matches its real-world presence, giving you a clear assessment before you invest in new media assets.

Learn More

Site Maintenance Audit

Ensure every media site remains in top condition. Our team inspects physical assets for damage, wear, visibility issues, or obstructions. Reports include geo-tagged visuals, timestamps, and detailed remarks to help maintain standards, ensure brand visibility, and take timely corrective actions.

Learn More
