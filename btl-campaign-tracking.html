BTL Campaign Tracking

  1. Home

Get the Full Picture of Your BTL Campaigns

BTL campaign tracking provides real-time monitoring and verification of all below-the-line marketing activities. Capture on-ground data from events, activations, and promotions to ensure compliance with campaign plans. We help you track deployment, measure effectiveness, and optimize future campaigns by delivering accurate, timely insights.

Validate BTL Activations in Real Time

Stay on top of every activation with real-time tracking and instant proof collection. Verify compliance, monitor performance, and catch issues before they impact results. This level of oversight drives efficiency and ensures your campaign delivers as planned.

  • Real-Time Activity Tracking

    Monitor the live progress of activations and events across multiple locations.

  • Proof of Deployment

    Collect photos, timestamps, and geo-coordinates as evidence of campaign execution.

  • Compliance Verification

    Ensure all brand guidelines and campaign requirements are met on-site.

  • Performance Reporting

    Generate detailed reports to analyze campaign effectiveness and identify improvement areas.

  • Multi-Location Coverage

    Track campaigns across standalone outlets, events, and retail chains seamlessly.

  • Automated Alerts

    Receive notifications for missed activities or non-compliance issues immediately.

End-to-End Oversight for Every BTL Activation

Manage BTL activations across multiple locations—from product samplings and in-store demos to trade shows, kiosks, and retail branding. Create detailed checklists for field agents, set custom survey questions to capture on-ground interactions, and get geotagged, time-stamped proof of every activity. All data is compiled in report-ready formats for swift stakeholder reviews and seamless internal sharing.

Monitor What Matters, Where It Matters

Track, Validate, and Monitor On-Ground Activities

Audit and real-time monitoring for BTL campaigns reduce risks of missed activations, inconsistent branding, and deployment errors. These measures improve campaign accuracy and effectiveness, helping brands maintain control and maximize ROI.

  • Detect missing or incorrect activations before they affect results.
  • Ensure promotional materials and setups meet brand standards.
  • Track BTL campaigns with regular status updates.
  • Improve accountability across field teams and vendors.
  • Use data-driven insights to optimize future campaigns.
